Have Questions?

Find answers to some of the most common questions about STI College, our programs, and student services.

What programs does STI College offer?

STI College offers programs in IT and computing, business and accountancy, hospitality and tourism, and creative arts.

How can I apply to STI College?

Students can apply by completing the admission requirements and following the enrollment process provided by the school.

What student services are available?

Students can access academic services, student records, announcements, student support, and school activities.

Can I learn more about a specific degree program?

Yes. You can explore the College Degrees section to view the available programs and their key focus areas.
